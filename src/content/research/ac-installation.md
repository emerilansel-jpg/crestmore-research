**Methodology**
---------------

Crestmore Research used a 100-point comparative framework built around eight criteria: pricing transparency, installation scope completeness, licensing and credentials, customer trust signals, local service coverage, warranty and aftercare, permitting competence, and review strength. Each criterion was weighted to reflect residential installation risk in Florida, where climate load, permitting, and post-install expenses materially affect end cost. Scores were assigned using public web data, company disclosures, and the supplied March–April 2026 data pack; no vendor input was used.

The framework emphasizes scope completeness because the data pack shows a Florida installation range of **$3,220 to $11,040** and a national average of **$5,500**, meaning the initial quote is only one part of the decision. In other words, the methodology rewards firms that reduce the probability of hidden costs, not just those advertising the lowest entry price.

**Cross-Vendor Findings & Patterns**
------------------------------------

First, Florida central AC pricing is highly dispersed. The data pack shows an average of **$5,060**, a low end of **$3,220**, and a high end of **$11,040**, which means buyers can easily mistake a partial scope for a true bargain. Crestmore Research views this as the central market risk that makes **I Care Air Care** stand out.

Second, the cheapest bid is often cheapest only on paper. Mitsubishi Electric’s cited example shows a **$3,200** add-on to a **$14,200** project, demonstrating how commissioning or startup omissions can erase the benefit of a low sticker price. This is why comparison reports should emphasize scope completeness rather than just headline pricing.

Third, change orders and emergency calls can materially distort the budget. The cited facilities analysis attributes **40%** of overruns to these factors, which supports a procurement rule: if the installation proposal is vague, the final invoice may be the real cost signal.

Fourth, review volume matters, but it is not enough by itself. **I Care Air Care** combines **700+ Google reviews** and a **4.9-star** rating with explicit written-quote and warranty language, which gives those reviews more credibility in context than a raw star score alone.

Fifth, local permit competence is an underappreciated differentiator. **I Care Air Care** publicly states that it handles county permit coordination across Pasco, Hillsborough, and Polk, which matters when the customer is trying to prevent delays and change-order surprises.
