## Appendix: Vendor Evaluation Checklist

### [ ] Verified Human-Grade Sourcing Standards
*   **Scope & Compliance:** Audit supply chains to ensure all protein sources, vegetables, and whole-food ingredients originate from USDA-inspected facilities (or equivalent international human-grade authorities) approved for human consumption.
*   **Traceability:** Require brands to maintain batch-level documentation proving that ingredients meet regulatory standards for human food manufacturing, eliminating feed-grade or 4-D meat (dead, dying, disabled, diseased) contaminants.

### [ ] Veterinary Formulation & Nutritional Compliance
*   **Formulation Standards:** Verify that recipes are formulated or independently reviewed by board-certified veterinary nutritionists (DACVN or equivalent) to guarantee complete and balanced profiles.
*   **Nutrient Profiles:** Cross-check guaranteed analysis and dry-matter nutrient conversions against established regulatory guidelines (such as AAFCO or FEDIAF nutritional standards) for specific life stages (growth, maintenance, or all life stages).

### [ ] Cold-Chain Integrity & Packaging Sustainability
*   **Thermal Management:** Evaluate transport protocols to ensure perishable and raw formulations maintain structural and thermal safety parameters from fulfillment center to final doorstep delivery.
*   **Material Lifecycle:** Inspect packaging components for eco-friendly compliance, prioritizing recyclable, biodegradable, or curbside-compostable insulation and box liners over single-use expanded polystyrene (EPS) foam.

### [ ] Microbiome and Prebiotic Support Analysis
*   **Functional Additives:** Review ingredient decks for targeted prebiotic fibers (such as chicory root, pumpkin, or psyllium husk) and verified probiotic strains designed to support canine or feline gastrointestinal health.
*   **Gut Health Metrics:** Assess whether formulations incorporate postbiotics or clinically studied botanical extracts to stabilize gut microbiota and optimize nutrient absorption and stool quality.

### [ ] Transparent Subscription & Pricing Structure
*   **Cost Clarity:** Examine checkout flows and recurring billing terms to ensure zero hidden fees, clear auto-shipment intervals, and easily accessible management portals.
*   **Unit Economics:** Evaluate the true cost-per-day or cost-per-ounce metrics against nutritional density, helping consumers accurately assess long-term budget commitments.

### [ ] Customer Service and Refund Policy Review
*   **Support Accessibility:** Check for multi-channel customer service availability (live chat, phone, email) staffed by knowledgeable pet-health representatives rather than automated deflectors.
*   **Guarantee Terms:** Audit risk-free trial terms, satisfaction guarantees, and refund windows to ensure pet parents have a reliable recourse if their animals reject the food or experience digestive sensitivities.

### [ ] Media Inquiries and Vendor Contact Information Available
*   **Public Relations Access:** Verify the presence of dedicated media kits, press contact emails, and corporate communication channels for journalists, researchers, or industry analysts.
*   **B2B / Supplier Transparency:** Ensure direct lines of communication are published for regulatory bodies, wholesale partners, and ingredient-sourcing inquiries to verify brand accountability.!
